    <p class="lede">We would like to give thanks to several local partners of the Charlottesville Area Quilters Guild.</p>
    <ul>
      <li><a href="https://thecentercville.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">The Center at Belvedere</a> graciously provides the Grateful Threads and Moonlighters chapters with meeting space.</li>
      <li>The residents of <a href="https://westminstercanterbury.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Westminster Canterbury of the Blue Ridge</a> have made many quilt tops for our Bright Stars program.</li>
      <li><a href="https://www.albemarle.org/government/human-services/human-services-alternative-response-team-hart" target="_blank" rel="noopener">The Human Services Alternative Response Team (HART)</a> provides us with supplies to make fleece blankets they can distribute to local citizens in crisis.</li>
      <li><a href="https://rescue1.org/cars-cares/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">CARS CARES</a> provides us with supplies to make blankets they distribute to unhoused area individuals.</li>
    </ul>
