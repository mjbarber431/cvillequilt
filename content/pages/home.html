Recreation

Our Guild has four chapters with regular meetings to promote fellowship among quilters in the Charlottesville, Virginia area. All skill levels and quilting styles are welcome. See Guild Chapters and About for more information.

Charity

We support a varity of local charitable initiatives including Bright Stars/Go to Grow preschool program. Meals on Wheels, Shelter for Help in Emergency and others. See Community Service for more information.

Education

We host yearly workshops with nationally known teachers and monthly mini-workshops taught by local Guild members. See our Calendar for more information.
