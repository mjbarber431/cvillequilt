Each year, the Crozet Chapter makes colorful pillowcases for the Shelter for Help in Emergency (SHE). SHE gives safe shelter to victims of domestic abuse. Children often use these as a pillow covering or a knapsack for their few possessions. Crozet invites all CAQG members to contribute to this cause. Chapter representatives will arrange delivery of pillowcases to the Crozet Chapter.

All fabrics should be pre-washed prior to constructing the pillowcases as they will not be washed again prior to distribution.
