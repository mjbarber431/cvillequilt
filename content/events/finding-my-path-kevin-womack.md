<p><strong>Finding My Path &#8211; A Trunk Show by Kevin Womack</strong></p>
<p>Join me on a tour of my colorful quilting journey as I share over 75 examples spanning more than 38 years. Starting from my humble beginnings in 1986 and traveling through precision piecing of traditional quilts, I soon developed a love for hand dyeing and patterning my own fabrics. Discover the story as I lead you down my personal path to becoming the artist that I am today.

See Kevin&#8217;s <a href="https://www.kevinwomackart.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener">website</a> for more details.</p>
