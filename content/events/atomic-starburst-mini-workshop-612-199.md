<p>Using the <em>Atomic Starburst</em> pattern by Violet Craft (<a href="https://www.violetcraft.com/atomic-starburst" target="_blank" rel="noopener">https://www.violetcraft.com/atomic-starburst</a>), you’ll learn the freezer paper method of paper piecing that eliminates the tedious removal of the paper from those sharp points.  You’ll also learn shortcuts to creating foundations and templates.  As an alternative to the quilt shown on the pattern, you can finish your project as a queen-sized quilt, a table runner or one of two sizes of Christmas tree skirt.  Limited to 16 participants.</p>
<p>Skill Level:  Confident Beginner<br>
Advanced prep:  Yes</p>
