In this workshop you will learn 3  different ways to piece curves.  We will be using Erin Grogan’s (Love Sew Modern) pattern Apparrotly it’s a Party.   By the end of the workshop you will have made a Parrot Block and a Flower Block.

Please note:  each person will need a copy of her pattern.  A link will be available upon registering for the workshop.

Also: you will not need to purchase a special ruler for this class.

Workshop fee:  $50 for members, $70 for non-members

Workshop is full – contact programs@cvillequilt.org to be added to the wait list. Each participant needs to complete an Erin Grogan registration form.
