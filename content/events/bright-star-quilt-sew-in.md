<p>There will be sewing/free motion quilting workshops to construct bright star and meals on wheels quilts. Chandra will teach the spiral technique. Robin will be available to teach other patterns. Please bring your sewing machine, extension cord, pins, scissors, thread, etc. as well as any unquilted Bright Star quilt tops. Robin will also have about 20 sets of Bright Star quilts (tops, backs and batting) for your use.</p>
