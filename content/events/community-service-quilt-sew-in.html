--- title: Community Service Quilt Sew-In start: 2025-10-30 13:00 end: 2025-10-30 16:00 category: Sew-In location: Cottonwood Quilt Shop address: 2035 Barracks Road, Charlottesville, VA organizer: Robin Hamill organizerEmail: rhamillruth@gmail.com ---

William will be there to help you use/learn any of the 3 sit-down long arm machines that
will be available. One of the large longarm machines will also be available to free
motion quilt your Bright Star of Meals on Wheels quilts.

He has also agreed to have several of the Bernina domestics available. If you’re
familiar with Bernina’s, these would be an option for you. If you have your own
domestic machine that you prefer to bring, please do. There will be a pinning table,
ironing boards and irons available.

Robin will bring about 20 kits for quilting if you want to do those. Some will have
generous backs that would work on the large long arm.

We’re hoping to set aside two afternoons a month to gather there and make this a
regular event.
