<p>Making a Christmas ornament by needle felting within the bounds of a cookie cutter is a great way to see what needle felting is all about. It’s also a safer way to start, since the cookie cutter offers some protection against accidental jabs. Participants in this three-hour workshop will make a gingerbread man ornament and decorate it!  All materials will be provided in a kit costing $8.00. Attendance is limited to 15.</p>
