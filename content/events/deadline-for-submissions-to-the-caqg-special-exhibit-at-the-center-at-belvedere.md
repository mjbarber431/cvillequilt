<p>A selection of &#8220;Seasons&#8221; themed quilts made by guild members will be displayed at the Center at Belvedere in September &#8211; October 2025. Our goal is to showcase our members’ work while educating the public about the broad range of methods, materials, and techniques used in today’s quilts. Maximum size: no more than 24” wide and no longer than 36” long.</p>
