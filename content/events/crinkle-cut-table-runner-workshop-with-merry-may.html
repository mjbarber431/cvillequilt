Crinkle Cut Table Runner

Make this stunning table runner with no special templates using a quilt-as-you-go technique.  NO BINDING.  This workshop explores shading to make a flat table runner look like it is 3-dimensional.  It is finished with a clever no-binding finish (can you imagine trying to bind all of those inside angles???).  Jelly Roll friendly.

About 22″ wide by about 42″ long.

Full-Day Workshop, 9:00 a.m. – 3:30 p.m.  At Ivy Creek Methodist Church.

Some cutting of fabric is required before class.  You will need a sewing machine and general sewing supplies.  Full supplies list will be provided to class registrants beforehand.  Sewing machine set-up starting at 8:30 a.m. with the workshop to begin at 9:00 a.m.

Maximum of 20 people.

For those with some experience in accurate piecing and cutting for the best results.

Registration is $30.00.  You will prompted to pay online using Square when you register.
